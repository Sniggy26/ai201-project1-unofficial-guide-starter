Georgia State University Student Reviews and Experiences

The following are real student reviews and experiences collected from Georgia State University students.

Academic Experience:
"Georgia State has really good programs, especially STEM programs. The faculty to student ratio is quite good. The professors are knowledgeable, friendly and approachable. You get really nice exposure to different kinds of classes where you are allowed to explore and choose your professors."

"The course curriculum is really nice and keeps up with industry standards. On top of that we have an option to engage in undergraduate research, something you don't generally see even in other good colleges."

"GSU is ranked #1 in the country for undergraduate teaching. It also has a reputation for being one of the most innovative universities in the country."

"So far my experience in computer science at Georgia State University has been really good. The faculty are good and they also provide you with sufficient study material in order to learn the concepts well. There are many research opportunities."

Campus Life:
"The campus life is definitely top notch. Downtown Atlanta is happening and you get events and activities all the time. You have pretty good places around and some really nice restaurants as well."

"Campus infrastructure is definitely one of the best ones I've seen. The libraries are up to date with modern technology. There are 3 free WiFi networks throughout the campus including a guest network."

"There are lots of sports activities and clubs. Students enjoy big events like Homecoming and Fall Fest. There are 500+ clubs, sports teams (Go Panthers!), arts shows, and volunteer groups — plenty to get involved, stay active, and have fun while studying."

"Georgia State University has 7 campuses across metro Atlanta: the main Atlanta Campus and 5 Perimeter campuses (Alpharetta, Clarkston, Decatur, Dunwoody, and Newton) plus Covington."

Concerns Students Have Raised:
"It's in downtown Atlanta. It can be a bit unsafe, and the university will take a while to release funds."

"As well as not having a small class ratio, which can impact how students interact with the professor."

"For an international student on an F1 visa, sometimes landing opportunities is not straightforward as GSU is still learning how to cater to the career needs of international students compared to other universities."

"Rent was $1,900 per month for an upscale apartment downtown." — This reflects the high cost of living near the GSU Atlanta campus.

Accommodation:
"I used online platforms like Discord to find roommates."
"I have a student apartment that I share with roommates."
"Students should be aware that downtown Atlanta apartments can be expensive — budget carefully."

Career and Internships:
"Downtown Atlanta provides students with unparalleled access to internships, cultural events, and career opportunities in Atlanta's thriving economy."

"GSU's location in the heart of Atlanta is a major advantage for students looking for internships in business, tech, healthcare, and media."

"Atlanta is home to many Fortune 500 companies — Delta, Coca-Cola, Home Depot, UPS — and GSU students often get internship opportunities at these companies."

General Advice from Students:
"Check PAWS regularly. Holds appear without warning and can block your registration."
"Get your MARTA UPass as soon as you arrive — it saves a lot of money on transportation."
"Use the campus library — it is modern, has lots of study spaces, and is open late."
"Join at least one student organization. It is the best way to make friends and build your network."
"Go to your professors' office hours. GSU professors are generally approachable and it makes a big difference in your grades."
"The Counseling and Testing Center is free and confidential — use it if you are struggling."
