Georgia State University Housing Guide

Georgia State University offers on-campus and affiliated housing for students in downtown Atlanta. Housing is managed through the MyHousing portal at myhousing.gsu.edu (also called STARREZ portal).

GSU On-Campus Housing Options:
GSU has several residence halls and apartment-style housing options in downtown Atlanta, including:
- University Commons: Apartment-style housing near campus
- Patton Hall: Traditional residence hall
- Greek housing options for students in fraternities and sororities

How to Apply for Housing:
1. Log in to the MyHousing portal at gsu.starrezhousing.com
2. Submit a housing application and pay the application fee
3. Wait for a housing assignment notification via your GSU student email
4. Check the MyHousing portal for your application status — do not email housing to check status, use the portal

Cost of Living at GSU:
On-campus housing in downtown Atlanta is expensive. Students report paying:
- On-campus residence hall: approximately $8,000 to $12,000 per academic year
- Off-campus apartments near campus: approximately $1,200 to $2,000+ per month
- One student reported paying $1,900/month for an upscale downtown Atlanta apartment

Most students use platforms like Discord, Facebook groups, and Roomies.com to find roommates for off-campus apartments.

Off-Campus Housing Tips:
- Start searching for off-campus housing early — the Atlanta rental market near GSU is competitive
- Proximity to a MARTA station is the most important factor if you don't have a car
- Check for proximity to the Blue/Green and Red/Gold MARTA lines
- Common neighborhoods for GSU students: downtown Atlanta, Midtown, Old Fourth Ward, East Atlanta Village
- Read lease agreements carefully — many Atlanta apartments charge separate fees for parking, pets, and amenities
- Use online platforms like Zillow, Apartments.com, and Facebook Housing groups to search

Safety Considerations for Off-Campus Housing:
- Downtown Atlanta has higher crime rates than suburban areas — research specific neighborhoods before signing a lease
- Check if the building has secure entry, cameras, and on-site management
- GSU's campus is in downtown Atlanta, which is urban — students should be aware of their surroundings at night
- The LiveSafe app can be used off-campus too for emergency contacts

Roommate Finding:
Many GSU students find roommates through:
- The unofficial GSU Discord server
- r/GeorgiaStateUniversity on Reddit
- GSU Facebook groups for incoming students
- The MyHousing portal's roommate matching feature

Important Housing Deadlines:
Housing assignments are made on a rolling basis — earlier applications get better options. Apply as early as possible for the best chance at your preferred building.

Contact: myhousing.gsu.edu or the University Housing office
