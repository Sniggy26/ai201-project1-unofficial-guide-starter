Georgia State University: Who to Contact for Help

Georgia State University has many offices and resources to help students. Knowing who to contact for which problem saves a lot of time.

Academic Problems:
- Questions about your major requirements or course planning: Contact your college's academic advisement office
- Questions about a grade or assignment: Contact your professor first, then the college dean if unresolved
- Academic dishonesty charge from a professor: First, request a meeting with your professor. If unresolved, contact the College Dean for the course to appeal.
- Academic probation or exclusion: Contact the University Advisement Center at advisement.gsu.edu

Financial Problems:
- Questions about financial aid, FAFSA, or loans: Contact the Office of Student Financial Aid via sfs.gsu.edu
- Questions about your tuition bill or student account: Contact Student Financial Services
- Hold on account related to finances: Check PAWS for the contact name listed on the hold, then email registrar@gsu.edu
- Scholarship questions: Contact the Scholarship Resource Center at success.students.gsu.edu

Registration and Enrollment:
- Dropped from classes: Contact the Enrollment Services Center immediately to find out why
- Questions about degree requirements: Use DegreeWorks in PAWS or contact your advisor
- Transfer credit questions: Check PAWS first under Student Records, then contact your college advisement office

Housing:
- Housing application status: Check myhousing.gsu.edu for updates or FAQs
- Housing problems or maintenance: Contact University Housing through the MyHousing portal

Technology Help:
- iCollege issues (course content, assignments, grades): Contact the Digital Learning Help Desk
- PAWS login or account issues: Contact the GSU Help Center at gsutech.service-now.com/sp
- GSU email (Panther Mail) problems: Contact IT support

Health and Wellness:
- Physical health needs: GSU Student Health Clinic
- Mental health support: GSU Counseling and Testing Center — free counseling for enrolled students
- Health insurance: Student Health Insurance information at sfs.gsu.edu

Safety and Emergencies:
- Campus emergency: Call 911 first, then GSU Police at 404-413-3333
- Non-emergency safety concern: Download the LiveSafe app — allows you to report suspicious activity and request a safety escort
- Get emergency alerts: Sign up at getrave.com/login/gsu so you receive GSU emergency text alerts

Student Rights and Conflict:
- Conflict with a professor or unfair treatment: Contact the Ombudsperson at ombuds.gsu.edu — they are a neutral resource who can help you understand your options
- Code of conduct questions: studenthandbook.gsu.edu

Panther Answers:
For many quick questions, use Panther Answers at gsu.my.site.com/support/s — it is GSU's official help portal where you can search for answers or submit a support ticket.
