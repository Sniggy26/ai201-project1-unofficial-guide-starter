Georgia State University MARTA Transportation Guide

Georgia State University participates in MARTA's University Pass (UPass) Program, which provides discounted transit options for students. GSU-issued MARTA Breeze Cards are customized for GSU and only work on MARTA bus and rail services. They are not valid for Xpress, CobbLinc, or Gwinnett County Transit.

MARTA Pass Pricing for GSU Students:
- Monthly Pass (Calendar Month): $61 via the Parking Portal
- This is significantly cheaper than the standard MARTA monthly pass price

How to Get Your Discounted MARTA Pass:
1. Log in to the GSU Parking Portal at parking.gsu.edu
2. Purchase your discounted monthly pass online
3. Wait for a confirmation email before going to pick up your card
4. Pick up your Breeze Card at the UBS Center on your campus
5. Do not visit the UBS Center until you receive your confirmation email — cards are not available until processing is complete

Important Notes:
- Funds are loaded onto your existing card; no new card is required after the initial card is issued
- University-purchased monthly passes include an extra discount
- This additional discount does NOT apply to 30-day passes
- Make sure to buy a Calendar Month pass, not a 30-day pass, to get the best deal

GSU Parking Portal: parking.gsu.edu
UBS Center location: Check your campus for the nearest UBS Center location

Getting Around Without a Car:
Most GSU students use MARTA to commute to campus. The closest MARTA stations to the downtown Atlanta campus are:
- Five Points Station (Red/Gold and Blue/Green lines)
- Peachtree Center Station (Red/Gold line)

Tips from students:
- Buy your monthly pass at the start of the calendar month, not mid-month, or you will waste money
- The Breeze Card tap readers are at every MARTA gate — tap in and tap out on buses
- MARTA runs until about midnight on weekdays and later on weekends
- Download the MARTA app or Google Maps to plan trips
- The MARTA train is by far the fastest way to get to and from Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta airport — much cheaper than Uber
