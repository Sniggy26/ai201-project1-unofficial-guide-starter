Georgia State University Academic Advising Guide

Academic advising at Georgia State University helps students plan their courses, stay on track for graduation, and navigate university policies. Every GSU student has access to academic advisors through their college or through the University Advisement Center.

New Student Advising:
New students admitted for fall and spring semesters should attend New Student Orientation. During orientation, students meet with academic advisors to plan their schedules and register for classes. New transfer students must also complete an online pre-orientation through the Transfer Advisement page before attending orientation.

How to Find Your Advisor:
Use the advisor search tool on the advisement website at advisement.gsu.edu to find your assigned advisor. Each college at GSU has its own advisement office:
- J. Mack Robinson College of Business: Business advisors
- College of Arts and Sciences: Arts and Sciences advisors
- Byrdine F. Lewis College of Nursing and Health Professions: Nursing advisors
- College of Education and Human Development: Education advisors

You can also use Panther Answers (gsu.my.site.com/support/s) for quick questions.

Drop/Add Period:
The drop/add period happens at the start of each semester. During this time, you can add or drop classes without academic penalty. After the drop/add period ends, dropping a class results in a W (withdrawal) on your transcript and you may still owe tuition for that class.

Checking Transfer Credit:
Log on to your PAWS account and look under Student Records, then select View Transfer Credit. This shows all transfer credits that have been evaluated and applied toward your degree. AP and IB credits also appear in this section. If you have questions about transfer credit evaluation, contact your college's advisement office directly.

Academic Probation and Reinstatement:
If your GPA falls below the minimum required for your program, you may be placed on academic probation. If you are excluded from the university, contact the University Advisement Center to discuss the procedures for reinstatement.

Tips for Working with Your Advisor:
- Schedule appointments early — advisors get very busy during registration periods
- Come prepared with a list of questions and a draft of your planned schedule
- Use DegreeWorks (accessible through PAWS) before your appointment so you already know which requirements you still need to fulfill
- If you are struggling in classes, see your advisor before midterms, not after — there are more options available earlier in the semester
- Email is often slow during peak periods; visit your college's advisement office in person if you have an urgent issue

Registration Tips:
- Registration opens at different times depending on your class standing — seniors register first, then juniors, etc.
- Check your registration time in PAWS and be ready to register exactly when your window opens for the best class selection
- Popular classes fill up within hours of registration opening
- If a class is full, add yourself to the waitlist and check for openings regularly during drop/add

Advisement Center Hours: Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 5:15 p.m.
