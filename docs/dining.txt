Georgia State University Dining Guide

Georgia State University's dining program is called PantherDining. GSU has multiple dining locations across its downtown Atlanta campus, plus additional options at Perimeter College campuses.

Main Dining Locations on the Atlanta Campus:
- The Commons: Main dining hall offering a wide variety of food options including a salad bar, hot entrees, international stations, and a dessert bar
- Urban Grille: Located in the Student Center, offers burgers, sandwiches, and grill items
- Panda Express: On campus for quick Chinese food options
- Chick-fil-A: On campus — one of the most popular spots, expect long lines during lunch
- Starbucks: Multiple locations on campus for coffee and snacks
- Subway: On campus for quick sandwiches
- Various food trucks and pop-up vendors rotate near the Student Center

Meal Plans:
GSU offers meal plan options for residential students. Meal plans typically include:
- A set number of meal swipes per week or semester for use at The Commons
- Dining Dollars (flex dollars) that can be spent at retail dining locations like Chick-fil-A, Panda Express, and others
- Commuter meal plan options for non-residential students who want the convenience of a plan

Tips on Meal Plans:
- Residential students in traditional dorms are often required to purchase a meal plan
- Dining Dollars roll over between fall and spring semesters but typically expire at the end of the academic year — spend them before May
- The Chick-fil-A on campus closes at certain hours and is closed Sundays — plan accordingly
- The Commons gets extremely crowded between noon and 1:30 PM on weekdays — go before 11:30 AM or after 2 PM to avoid long waits
- Meal swipes at The Commons give you all-you-can-eat access — take advantage of this

Off-Campus Dining Near GSU:
Downtown Atlanta has excellent food options near campus:
- Centennial Olympic Park area has several restaurants
- Peachtree Street corridor has affordable lunch spots popular with students
- MARTA can take you to Midtown and other neighborhoods with diverse and affordable restaurant options
- Ponce City Market (accessible by MARTA) is popular for weekend meals
- Students recommend exploring Underground Atlanta and Broad Street for affordable local eats

Student Food Budget Tips:
- Grocery stores near campus include Publix and Kroger, accessible via MARTA
- Many students cook at home to save money, especially those in apartment-style housing with kitchens
- The GSU Food Pantry (Paw's Pantry) provides free food assistance to students in need — no questions asked, no judgment
- Paw's Pantry is located in the Student Center — any currently enrolled student can use it

PantherCard:
Your PantherCard (student ID) is linked to your dining account. You use it to swipe for meals at The Commons and to spend Dining Dollars at retail locations. You can also add money to your PantherCard account online.
