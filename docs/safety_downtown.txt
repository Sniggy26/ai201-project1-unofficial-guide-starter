Georgia State University Safety Guide: Staying Safe in Downtown Atlanta

Georgia State University's main campus is located in downtown Atlanta, an urban environment that requires students to be aware of their surroundings. GSU provides several safety resources to help students stay safe on and near campus.

GSU Safety Resources:

LiveSafe App:
The LiveSafe app is GSU's official safety app. Download it for free on iOS or Android. With LiveSafe you can:
- Send a tip to GSU Police about suspicious activity
- Request a safety escort if you need to walk somewhere at night
- Virtually walk with a friend so someone knows your location
- Contact emergency services with one tap
- Receive safety alerts from GSU

Download LiveSafe: safety.gsu.edu/livesafe

Emergency Alerts:
Sign up for GSU emergency text alerts at getrave.com/login/gsu. These alerts notify you immediately of emergencies on or near campus — severe weather, active threats, or major incidents.

GSU Police:
- Emergency: 911
- Non-emergency GSU Police: 404-413-3333
- GSU Police patrols the downtown campus 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
- Police escort service is available at night — call or use the LiveSafe app

Walking Safety Tips for GSU Students:
- Avoid walking alone at night whenever possible
- Stick to well-lit, populated paths — Peachtree Street, Edgewood Avenue, and the main campus corridors are better than side streets
- Be aware of your surroundings — avoid looking at your phone while walking
- Trust your instincts — if something feels unsafe, cross the street, enter a building, or call for help
- Walk with confidence and purpose
- Keep earbuds out or at low volume so you can hear your environment
- The GSU library, Student Center, and main buildings are open late and are safe places to wait if you feel unsafe

MARTA Safety Tips:
- Be aware of your surroundings on MARTA platforms, especially late at night
- Stand back from the edge of the platform when trains are approaching
- Sit near the front of the train or near other passengers when riding late at night
- Do not display expensive items like phones, laptops, or jewelry openly on MARTA
- Five Points Station is a major hub and can get chaotic during rush hour — hold onto your belongings

General Downtown Atlanta Awareness:
- Downtown Atlanta has areas of higher crime — stay on main corridors and well-lit streets
- Avoid Forsyth Street and certain blocks south of campus late at night
- The area around Centennial Olympic Park and the Georgia Aquarium is generally well-trafficked and safer
- Do not leave bags or laptops unattended in public spaces, libraries, or cafes
- Lock your bike with a U-lock if you cycle to campus

What to Do in an Emergency:
1. Call 911 for any immediate threat to life or safety
2. Then call GSU Police at 404-413-3333
3. Move to a safe indoor location if possible
4. Follow instructions from GSU emergency alerts
5. Alert classmates and friends

Campus Blue Light Emergency Phones:
Blue light emergency phones are located throughout the GSU downtown campus. Press the button to instantly connect to GSU Police. Know where your nearest one is.

Student Mental Health and Safety:
If you or a classmate is in a mental health crisis, contact:
- GSU Counseling and Testing Center: 404-413-1640
- Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
- National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988
